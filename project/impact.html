What litter actually costs

A dropped cup or wrapper looks small. Multiplied across a neighborhood over a year, it adds up to real harm — to wildlife, waterways, and the people who live there.

Wildlife

Birds, fish, and small mammals mistake plastic fragments, cigarette filters, and food packaging for food. Six-pack rings and tangled debris also trap and injure animals directly.

Waterways

Storm drains connect directly to local creeks and rivers. Litter dropped on a street rarely stays there — wind and rain move it into the drainage system, where it eventually reaches open water and breaks down into microplastics that are nearly impossible to remove.

Community

Litter-heavy areas see lower foot traffic, lower property values, and a measurable drop in how safe residents say they feel in their own neighborhood — even when actual crime rates haven't changed.
