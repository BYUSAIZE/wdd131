Site Plan: Street Sweep

WDD 131 — Project Site Plan

Site Name

Street Sweep

The name is short, active, and describes exactly what the site asks visitors to do about littering: sweep it off the street. It avoids vague environmental buzzwords and instead points to a concrete, local action — picking up litter in your own neighborhood.

Optional domain: streetsweep.community

Site Purpose

Street Sweep raises awareness about the causes and effects of littering in public spaces and gives visitors a clear path to act: it explains why litter is a problem for wildlife, waterways, and neighborhoods, and it connects visitors to cleanup events and a volunteer sign-up form so awareness turns into participation.

Scenarios

  • "I keep seeing trash pile up near the river by my house — what actual harm does that do, and who is responsible for cleaning it up?"
  • "I want to help but I don't know where to start — is there a cleanup event near me, and how do I sign up?"
  • "I run a small community group and want to organize our own cleanup — what does Street Sweep recommend for planning one?"

Color Schema

The palette borrows from municipal street signage — the colors people already associate with public streets and civic notices — rather than a generic "eco" green-on-white look. It is applied throughout this very document.

Typography

Two fonts, each doing one job. Both are applied on this page.

Headings — Oswald, weight 600–700
Every piece of litter has somewhere to go
Body text — Source Sans 3, weight 400/600

Oswald's condensed, upright letterforms echo the stenciled type used on street signs and curb markings, giving headings a civic, no-nonsense tone. Source Sans 3 is used for all body copy, form labels, and navigation because it stays highly legible at small sizes on mobile screens.
