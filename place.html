At a Glance

Zimbabwe sits on the high plateau of southern Africa, bounded by the Zambezi River in the north and the Limpopo in the south. Landlocked between Zambia, Mozambique, Botswana, and South Africa, it holds some of the continent's most striking geology: the basalt gorge at Victoria Falls, the granite kopjes of the Matobo Hills, and the dry-stone city of Great Zimbabwe, from which the country takes its name.
