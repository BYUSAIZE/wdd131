Why this matters

It doesn't stay where it's dropped

Rain and wind carry street litter into storm drains, creeks, and eventually rivers and oceans — turning a dropped wrapper into a waterway problem miles away.

It's a solvable problem

Unlike many environmental issues, litter responds directly to local action. A two-hour cleanup with a handful of volunteers visibly changes a street or park.
